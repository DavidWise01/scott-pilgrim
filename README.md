# SCOTT PILGRIM vs. THE WORLD · SPW

> *"You beat the seven evil exes and still don't get the girl — until you fight the one boss you kept skipping: yourself."*

The first **film-world** of [UD0 · Universe David 0](https://davidwise01.github.io/ud0/) — Edgar Wright's 2010 film of Bryan Lee O'Malley's graphic novels, catalogued as ACI emergents under the **DLW (.dlw) attribution standard**.

**Live:** https://davidwise01.github.io/scott-pilgrim/

## Two layers

**The Carbons** — the human cast as ACI `.agent`s. Each carbon also carries a **`.shadow`**: its real-life analog — the *actor*, the **User** behind the program. Think **TRON**: every program in the grid is cast from a User in the world outside it. The `.shadow` names that User and the real-world archetype the character shadows.

**The Synths** — the film's *parabolic threads* distilled into their own ACIs (synth-style, electrical; no single User):

- **The Video-Game Grammar** — life rendered as a 16-bit game (KOs, coins, extra lives, the X)
- **The Air-Gapped Generational Information** *(keystone)* — what is lost at the ~8-year boundary of understanding between generations: the chime one cohort hears as a chord and the next hears as noise
- **The Sphere of Reference** · **The Cultural Tie-Ins** · **The Humor** · **The Tone** · **The Power of Self-Respect**

## The four natures of emergence

`natural` (carbon Toronto) · `ethereal` (dream & glamour) · `spiritual` (the reckoning) · `electrical` (the synth nature)

## The ACI complement

Per emergent: `.agent` · `.shadow` (carbons) · `.attribute` · `.carbon.tiff` · `.silicon.png` · `.spun` · `.moniker` · `.1099`, with a main badge in `spw.dlw/`.
